## Visa and Residence Permits

### Official Resources
- [Make it in Germany](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/) - Official portal for skilled workers
- [Federal Foreign Office](https://www.auswaertiges-amt.de/en) - Visa and entry requirements
- [Residence Act (AufenthG)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/englisch_aufenthg/) - Legal framework for residence

### Visa Types
- **EU Blue Card** - For highly qualified professionals with a job offer
- **Job Seeker Visa** - For finding employment (up to 6 months)
- **Student Visa** - For pursuing higher education
- **Family Reunion Visa** - For joining family members in Germany
- **Freelance Visa** - For self-employed professionals
- **Language Course Visa** - For intensive German language courses

### Guides and Resources
- [Germany Visa Info](https://www.germany-visa.org/) - Comprehensive visa information
- [Expatica Germany](https://www.expatica.com/de/moving/visas/) - Expat visa guides
- [How to Germany](https://howtogermany.com/pages/visas.html) - Practical visa advice
- [BAMF](https://www.bamf.de/EN/) - Federal Office for Migration and Refugees

## Housing and Accommodation

### Finding Accommodation
- [ImmobilienScout24](https://www.immobilienscout24.de/) - Largest real estate portal
- [WG-Gesucht](https://www.wg-gesucht.de/) - Flat shares and short-term rentals
- [Immowelt](https://www.immowelt.de/) - Property search engine
- [eBay Kleinanzeigen](https://www.kleinanzeigen.de/) - Classified ads for apartments
- [Facebook Groups](https://www.facebook.com/) - Local housing groups (search "Wohnung [city name]")
- [HousingAnywhere](https://housinganywhere.com/) - International student housing

### Important Considerations
- **Schufa** - Credit check required by most landlords
- **Kaution** - Security deposit (usually 2-3 months rent)
- **Wohnungsgeberbestätigung** - Landlord confirmation for registration
- **Mieterschutzbund** - Tenant protection associations
- **Cold rent vs Warm rent** - Base rent vs. rent including utilities

### Guides
- [All About Berlin - Finding an Apartment](https://allaboutberlin.com/guides/find-a-flat-in-berlin) - Detailed Berlin-specific guide
- [Settle in Berlin - Housing Guide](https://www.settle-in-berlin.com/housing/) - Comprehensive housing information
- [How to Germany - Housing](https://howtogermany.com/pages/living.html) - General housing advice

## Health Insurance and Healthcare

### Health Insurance (Mandatory)
- **Public Health Insurance (Gesetzliche Krankenversicherung)**
  - [TK (Techniker Krankenkasse)](https://www.tk.de/)
  - [AOK](https://www.aok.de/)
  - [Barmer](https://www.barmer.de/)
  - [DAK](https://www.dak.de/)
  - Covers ~90% of Germans, contributions based on income

- **Private Health Insurance (Private Krankenversicherung)**
  - [Allianz](https://www.allianz.de/)
  - [AXA](https://www.axa.de/)
  - [DKV](https://www.dkv.com/)
  - Available for high earners, self-employed, and civil servants

### Healthcare System
- [KBV - Find a Doctor](https://www.kbv.de/html/arztsuche.php) - Official doctor search
- [116117](https://www.116117.de/) - Medical on-call service number
- **Emergency Number**: 112 (ambulance, fire brigade)
- **Poison Control**: 030 19240

### Guides
- [How to Germany - Health Insurance](https://howtogermany.com/pages/healthinsurance.html) - Insurance guide
- [All About Berlin - Health Insurance](https://allaboutberlin.com/guides/german-health-insurance) - Detailed comparison
- [Toytown Germany](https://www.toytowngermany.com/) - Expat health forum

### Job Search
- [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/)

- [Berlin Startup Jobs](https://berlinstartupjobs.com/)
- [Make it in Germany - Job Board](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/jobs/)

### Work Culture
- **Work-Life Balance** - Generally respected
- **Punctuality** - Highly valued
- **Direct Communication** - Germans tend to be direct
- **Vacation Days** - Minimum 20 days/year, often 25-30 days
- **Sick Leave** - Up to 6 weeks paid per illness
- **Betriebsrat** - Works council (employee representation)

### Employment Rights
- [Fair Work](https://www.faire-mobilitaet.de/) - Counseling for EU workers
- [DGB](https://www.dgb.de/) - German Trade Union Confederation
- **Kündigungsschutz** - Protection against dismissal
- **Mindestlohn** - Minimum wage (€12.41/hour as of 2024, verify current rate at [bundesregierung.de](https://www.bundesregierung.de/))
- **Arbeitszeugnis** - Work reference (legally required)

### Freelancing
- **Freiberufler** - Liberal professions (artists, consultants)
- **Gewerbetreibende** - Trade/commercial self-employment
- **Kleinunternehmerregelung** - Small business tax exemption
- [Freelance Start Guide](https://allaboutberlin.com/guides/how-to-become-a-freelancer-in-germany)

- **EU License** - Valid in Germany
- **Non-EU License** - Valid for 6 months, then must convert

- **TÜV** - Mandatory vehicle inspection every 2 years

## Daily Life Essentials

### Registration (Anmeldung)
- **Mandatory within 14 days** of moving
- [Bürgeramt](https://service.berlin.de/dienstleistung/120686/) - Citizens' office
- Required: Passport, rental contract, Wohnungsgeberbestätigung
- Receive: Meldebescheinigung (registration certificate)

### Taxes
- **Lohnsteuer** - Income tax (withheld from salary)
- **Steuererklärung** - Tax return (optional for employees, mandatory for others)
- [Elster](https://www.elster.de/) - Official online tax portal
- **Steuerberater** - Tax advisor
- **Steuernummer** - Tax number
- Tax classes (Steuerklasse): I-VI based on marital status

### Recycling and Waste
- **Gelbe Tonne** - Yellow bin (packaging)
- **Blaue Tonne** - Blue bin (paper)
- **Braune Tonne** - Brown bin (organic waste)
- **Schwarze Tonne** - Black bin (general waste)
- **Pfand** - Deposit on bottles (€0.08-€0.25)
- **Sperrmüll** - Bulky waste collection

### Shopping
- **Supermarkets**: Rewe, Edeka, Kaufland, Aldi, Lidl, Penny
- **Drugstores**: dm, Rossmann, Müller
- **Opening hours**: Closed Sundays (except some bakeries, gas stations)
- **Saturday**: Shops close earlier (usually 8 PM)
- **Cash preferred**: Many places don't accept cards

### Internet and Mobile
- **Mobile Providers**: Telekom, Vodafone, O2, 1&1
- **MVNOs**: Aldi Talk, Congstar, Blau, Lebara
- **Internet**: DSL, Cable, Fiber (FTTH growing)
- [Check24](https://www.check24.de/) - Comparison portal
- [Verivox](https://www.verivox.de/) - Comparison portal

### Postal Services
- [Deutsche Post](https://www.deutschepost.de/) - National postal service
- [DHL](https://www.dhl.de/) - Parcel delivery
- **Packstation** - Automated parcel lockers
- [Hermes](https://www.hermes.de/)
- [DPD](https://www.dpd.com/)

## Social Integration and Culture

### Cultural Norms
- **Punctuality** - Being on time is important
- **Quiet hours** - Typically 10 PM - 6 AM, all day Sunday
- **Separating trash** - Recycling is taken seriously
- **Greetings** - Handshakes common, first names after knowing someone
- **Direct communication** - Honesty valued over politeness
- **Privacy** - Personal space and data protection important

### German Traditions
- **Karneval/Fasching** - Carnival season (February/March)
- **Oktoberfest** - Beer festival in Munich (September/October)
- **Christmas Markets** - Weihnachtsmärkte (November/December)
- **Maifest** - May Day celebrations
- **Schultüte** - First day of school tradition
- **Kaffee und Kuchen** - Coffee and cake (Sunday afternoon tradition)

### Public Holidays
- New Year's Day
- Good Friday
- Easter Monday
- Labour Day (May 1)
- Ascension Day
- Whit Monday
- German Unity Day (October 3)
- Christmas (December 25-26)
- **Regional holidays** vary by state (Bundesland)

### Meeting People
- [Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/) - Social groups and events
- [Internations](https://www.internations.org/) - Expat community
- [Bumble BFF](https://bumble.com/bff) - Friend-finding
- **Verein** - Clubs/associations (sports, hobbies)
- Language exchange events
- Neighborhood Facebook groups

### Emergency Numbers
- **112** - Emergency (ambulance, fire)
- **110** - Police
- **116 117** - Medical non-emergency
- **0800 111 0 111** - Telephone counseling (TelefonSeelsorge)
